Scientists Put Young Cells Into Adult Brains. They Replaced Them.

@octopusinc_ · 2026-08-11 · Bio Gen

This video discusses research on transplanting young glial progenitor cells into the brains of elderly mice. The key finding is that these young cells not only repair damage but competitively replace existing aged and diseased human glia in the adult chimeric mouse brain. This research explores potential avenues for extending brain lifespan and treating neurodegenerative diseases by replacing dysfunctional cells, though ethical concerns and practical limitations for human application are also addressed.
