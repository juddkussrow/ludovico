It is smaller and lighter than the Meta Quest 3, with a battery pack on the back and a visor on the front for better weight distribution. An optional $59 ergonomic pack includes a head strap and controller grips. The headset has a 2160x2160 per-eye LCD display with a refresh rate up to 144 Hz, though many VR games default to 72 Hz. Approximately 100 Valve-verified VR games are available, mostly ports of existing Quest titles. Half-Life: Alyx is playable in a beta build, though with reduced graphics and frame rates compared to PC VR. The controllers are covered in capacitive touch sensors, allowing for finger motion tracking, and mimic Steam controller button layouts. The pass-through cameras are black and white, but a $150 Arcturus Vision camera module can be added for high-resolution color pass-through, spatial video recording, and 3D scanning. The Steam Frame is modular, with detachable faceplates, straps, and battery. It runs Linux, allowing access to a desktop mode for running applications like Firefox and sideloading. While it can run many Steam games, performance varies, with some graphically intense games like Elden Ring being unplayable. Battery life is poor, lasting only about two hours.
