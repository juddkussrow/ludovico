DBX Is Almost the Perfect Free Database Client

@octopusinc_ · 2026-09-08 · DevOps

This video reviews DBX, a free and open-source database client that supports over 90 database engines and integrates with AI coding agents via its MCP server. While praising its lightweight architecture and agent workflow, the video highlights a critical security flaw: DBX stores saved database credentials in plaintext within its local `dbx.db` SQLite database. The creator demonstrates setting up DBX with Claude and compares it to alternatives like DataGrip and Navicat, ultimately recommending caution due to the security vulnerability.
