@octopusinc_ · 2025-08-19 · Hardware

This video discusses how Tesla EVs can now perform vehicle-to-home (V2H) and vehicle-to-grid (V2G) functions without needing a Powerwall, primarily through the use of third-party bidirectional DC chargers like those from SIG Energy. These chargers bypass the car's onboard system, allowing Teslas and other EVs to power homes or feed electricity back into the grid, potentially generating income for owners. The video highlights the SIG Energy system's features, including its stackable design and different power output options, and notes its compatibility with various EV brands, though with some caveats for General Motors vehicles.
