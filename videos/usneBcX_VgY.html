The core of Animotive's motion capture relies on a performer wearing a VR headset, such as the Meta Quest Pro, which tracks head, eye, and facial movements, as well as controller positions. To extrapolate full-body movements from these limited data points, Animotive developed its own AI solution. This AI was trained on approximately 15 hours of high-quality Vicon motion capture data, covering various movements like walking at different speeds and changing direction. This extensive dataset allows the AI to make natural assumptions about leg movements and overall body posture, surpassing the "ropey" results of traditional inverse kinematics (IK). While Animotive is compatible with professional motion capture suits like Xsens and Vicon for higher quality, its AI-driven approach enables use in more accessible environments like bedrooms or classrooms. A key differentiator is its real-time processing, avoiding delays common in video-based solutions, which is crucial for embodied performance. Animotive addresses the challenge of retargeting motion to characters with different proportions by having the performer experience the virtual world from the character's perspective, ensuring spatial accuracy and reducing post-production cleanup. The platform exports all data, including characters, sets, props, and animations, as spatially accurate FBX files, which can be smoothly imported into Maya, Blender, Unity, or Unreal. This eliminates the need for manual reassembly and retargeting in external 3D tools. Collaboration can occur in a shared physical room or remotely, though in-person collaboration is preferred by many production studios. Animotive also supports asynchronous workflows, allowing users to layer performances and camera movements piece by piece, with pre-edited audio aiding synchronization.
