This video demonstrates a workflow for creating 3D Gaussian Splats using footage from the Antigravity A1 360° panoramic drone. The process involves exporting 360 video via Antigravity Studio, extracting multi-angle still frames using the 360 Extractor add-on in Blender, and then processing these frames in Postshot to generate the 3D Gaussian Splat. The creator provides practical tips on drone flight altitude and frame step settings to optimize the final splat quality, which is then viewed in the Super Splat editor.
