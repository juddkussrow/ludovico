The Rise and Fall of Music NFTs

This video analyzes the rise and fall of music NFTs and Web3 platforms from 2021 to 2026, based on a Chartlex article. It highlights that most music NFT projects and platforms, such as Royal.io and OneOf, collapsed or pivoted due to a lack of genuine fan adoption and over-reliance on speculation. The analysis concludes that traditional platforms like Bandcamp, Patreon, and Substack, alongside direct email, proved more sustainable for artist monetization.
