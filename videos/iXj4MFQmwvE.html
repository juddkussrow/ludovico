TRISO, standing for tri-structural isotropic, is a nuclear fuel particle that encapsulates uranium with layers of carbon and ceramic, creating a hermetically sealed containment system. This design makes the fuel extremely robust and inherently safe, preventing fission gas release and making reactors incapable of severe accidents like Fukushima. Standard Nuclear aims to be the primary supplier of TRISO fuel for a new generation of Small Modular Reactors (SMRs), which are gaining traction due to their safety and portability. The manufacturing process involves dissolving uranium feedstock in acid to create a "broth," forming gel spheres, and then coating them in a furnace where they glow red-hot to ensure even coating. These TRISO particles are then embedded into various shapes, such as silicon carbide or graphite matrix pellets, to form fuel elements. The video emphasizes that China has been building TRISO reactors for years, and the US is now trying to reclaim its leadership in this technology. The potential applications for these SMRs, powered by TRISO fuel, include powering AI data centers, military bases, and even urban areas, offering a solution for clean, decentralized energy. Standard Nuclear's goal is to democratize TRISO fuel production to meet the growing demand from new reactor designs.
