World Labs released Atlas, a new world model that enables high-precision 3D reconstruction from a significantly reduced number of images (50-100x fewer), as demonstrated by a multi-room capture from only 30 images and a Stanford campus reconstruction from ground-level photos. ByteDance is also exploring 4D media systems. The video also mentions three 4D Gaussian splatting films at the Venice Biennale, processed by 4DVAI, including "Out of the Ashes" documenting the Eaton wildfires.
