This Tiny AI Never Stops Learning (mini-AGI)

@octopusinc_ · 2026-09-30 · Bio Gen, DevOps, Favorites, Story

This video introduces mini-AGI, an open-source language model designed for continuous learning on consumer hardware. Unlike traditional models, mini-AGI stores experts as separate files on disk, allowing it to scale beyond GPU memory and avoid catastrophic forgetting. The creator demonstrates training it on an M2 Max MacBook Pro, first with children's stories to learn English, then with Quentin Tarantino screenplays to adapt its style, showcasing its ability to learn new information without forgetting old knowledge.
