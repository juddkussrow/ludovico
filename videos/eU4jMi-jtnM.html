How to Create 3D Models with the Insta360 X6 & X5 Using Spatial Capture

@octopusinc_ · 2026-08-23 · Hardware, ListAssist

This video demonstrates how to use the "Spatial Capture" feature within the Insta360 app to create 3D models from 360-degree video recordings made with Insta360 X6, X5, and X4 Air cameras. The process involves recording a 360 video, uploading it to the Insta360 cloud, and then using the app to initiate the 3D rendering, which is based on 3D Gaussian Splatting. The resulting 3D models can be explored within the app or shared via a web link.
