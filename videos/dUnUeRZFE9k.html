This video demonstrates how the Kia EV9's Vehicle-to-Load (V2L) feature can power a travel trailer, eliminating the need for a noisy generator. The EV9, with its 99 kWh battery, provides up to 15 amps (1.8 kW) through its outlets, sufficient for running essential RV appliances like the fridge, lights, and even a microwave, provided only one high-power item is used at a time. The V2L functionality automatically stops when the car's battery reaches 20% to preserve driving range.
