I Invented Digital Film

@octopusinc_ · 2026-08-28 · Hardware

This video showcases the creation of a "digital film" instant camera that uses removable, reusable e-paper displays as physical photo cartridges. The camera, built around a Raspberry Pi and a camera module, processes images with a custom Python script to adapt them to the e-paper's four-color limitation. The project emphasizes the physical tangibility of photos without traditional printing, offering a unique blend of digital convenience and analog feel.
