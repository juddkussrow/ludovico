Karmann Ghia electric conversion kit

@octopusinc_ · 2026-08-07 · Hardware

This video showcases a bolt-in electric conversion kit for the Karmann Ghia, designed by Electric Classic Cars. The kit includes a 200kW motor, a 42kWh battery pack split between front and rear, and supports CCS (Type 1 or 2) or AC charging. It features custom components like wiring looms, brackets, and a control console, and is built to ECE R100 Rev 1 safety standards, including an inertia switch for crash detection.
