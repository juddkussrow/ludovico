AudioMuse-AI: Free Open Source Alternative to Spotify AI DJ

@octopusinc_ · 2026-07-10 · Music Gen

AudioMuse-AI is a free, open-source, self-hosted playlist generator that performs sonic analysis on local music libraries to create AI-powered playlists and discovery features. It integrates with media servers like Jellyfin, Navidrome, LMS, Lyrion, and Emby, and can be deployed via Docker Compose or managed services like Elestio. The tool enables users to explore music through features like Song Alchemy, Instant Playlists, Music Map, and text/lyrics search, all without relying on external metadata or APIs.
