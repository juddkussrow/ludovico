Claude Skill Turns Your Repo Into an Architecture Diagram

@octopusinc_ · 2026-09-06 · DevOps

Archify is a Claude Skill that generates interactive architecture diagrams from code repositories. It focuses on creating verifiable diagrams by linking components to actual files and commits, rather than just drawing pictures. The tool validates diagrams against a schema and performs checks, including verifying the existence of cited source files, to ensure accuracy and prevent misrepresentation of system architecture.
