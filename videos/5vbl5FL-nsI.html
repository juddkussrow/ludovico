The Open Source Internet Is Here

@octopusinc_ · 2026-09-20 · Hardware

This video explores building an open-source internet using amateur radio, specifically focusing on bridging the Reticulum network onto High Frequency (HF) bands. The creator demonstrates how to assemble and test a Hermes Lite 2 software-defined radio, tune antennas using a NanoVNA, and receive long-distance data packets (FT8) and voice broadcasts. The ultimate goal is to send a digital message hundreds of miles without traditional infrastructure, leveraging the ionosphere for skywave propagation.
