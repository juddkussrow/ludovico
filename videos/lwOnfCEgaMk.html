Johns Hopkins reportedly created a brain organoid comparable to a 40-day-old fetus, featuring multiple brain regions and 80% of target cells. The next goal is a 1 cm organoid, mouse-brain sized. The video highlights that organoids can be connected via axons for more complex signaling, behaving as one unit, and can be trained by AI or direct AI. A major challenge for larger organoids is hypoxia due to the lack of a vascular system, which is being addressed by combining them with vascular organoids or creating two-dimensional organoids. Another solution is implanting them into mice, where they integrate and develop brain waves as complex as the mouse's brain. This integration has shown promise in treating conditions like strokes and Parkinson's disease in animal models.
