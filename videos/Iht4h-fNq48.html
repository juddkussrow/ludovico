The core innovation is shifting the safety layer from a vendor's fixed taxonomy to a user-defined configuration. The architecture involves a "data plane" for processing requests through gates and a "control plane" where policy is defined via an instruct field (strict, moderate, lenient), a query (yes/no question), and a threshold. The video highlights the governance challenge: while users gain control over policy wording and thresholds, these are not versioned by default, making it difficult to track changes and attribute shifts in flagged content rates.
