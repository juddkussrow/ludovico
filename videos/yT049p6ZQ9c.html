This video introduces Antithesis, a tool designed to find and reproduce bugs in distributed systems. It operates by running systems in deterministic virtual machines, injecting faults, and providing detailed reports with reproducible timelines, logs, and a "Multiverse Debugger" for interactive debugging. The tool aims to help teams ship bug-free distributed systems by identifying deep-seated issues before they reach production.
