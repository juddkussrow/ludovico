AlphaGenome Atlas: Understanding the human genome

@octopusinc_ · 2026-09-08 · Bio Gen

AlphaGenome Atlas is a DeepMind product that provides a comprehensive dataset and web platform for predicting the molecular impact of every possible single-letter change in the human genome. It uses an AlphaGenome Variant Impact (AVI) score to quantify the potential deleteriousness of genetic variants, making this complex genomic information accessible to researchers. The platform aims to accelerate genetic research by simplifying the analysis of billions of potential mutations.
