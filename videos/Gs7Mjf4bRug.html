I Reverse-Engineered an $8 LiDAR and Built a Wireless 360° Radar

@octopusinc_ · 2026-08-03 · Hardware

This video details the reverse-engineering of an inexpensive rotating LiDAR sensor and its integration with an HLK-LD2450 24 GHz mmWave sensor to create a wireless 360° radar system. The system uses an ESP32-S3 to process sensor data and transmit it wirelessly to a custom Android application for display. The creator demonstrates the assembly of the portable sensor unit and tests its functionality outdoors.
