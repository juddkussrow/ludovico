The Hidden Cost of "Clean" Design

@octopusinc_ · 2025-12-05 · DevOps

This video argues that modern "clean" design, exemplified by Apple Music and Google Docs, sacrifices personality and character for efficiency and simplicity, leading to bland and forgettable user experiences. It contrasts this with older software like Winamp and Windows XP, which, despite technical flaws, were memorable due to their distinctive, author-driven designs. The creator suggests that the shift to minimalist design reflects a societal change in priorities rather than an inevitable technological evolution.
