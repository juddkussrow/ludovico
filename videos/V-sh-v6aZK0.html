The Anywhere Network is Here

@octopusinc_ · 2026-08-30 · Hardware, Long Form

This video details the construction and field testing of a Reticulum Iridium relay bridge using a RockBLOCK 9704 satellite modem and a Raspberry Pi Zero. The goal was to enable two-way messaging via Reticulum over Iridium from a remote mountain location without cell service. While uplink messages were successfully received by the server, live round-trip communication failed in the field due to environmental factors and software limitations, though it worked under open skies at home.
