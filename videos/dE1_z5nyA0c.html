He emphasizes the shift from phones to AR glasses, highlighting Snap's decade-long investment in AR technology, including developer tools, operating systems, displays, and optics. The presentation showcases the developer community, with over 450,000 developers building 5 million lenses using Snap's AR tools. Lens Studio, originally for Snapchat, is now a powerful developer environment for immersive experiences, featuring scene understanding, spatial mapping, connected lenses, and Snap Cloud for dynamic experiences. THE MIGRATION AGENT and a Native Development Kit (NDK) are announced to facilitate bringing existing projects from tools like Unity into Lens Studio, with Mapbox porting their navigation engine in under 2 hours using the NDK. SPECS are described as lightweight (132g for 47mm frame, 136g for 52mm frame), made from Swiss TR90 polymer, and support prescription lenses. They feature a proprietary optical engine with a liquid crystal on silicon display, a waveguide with nanostructures for a 51-degree field of view (equivalent to a 24-inch desktop display or 115-inch home cinema screen at 10 feet), and 16 million colors. Electrochromic lenses shift from clear to tinted in 10 seconds, with a 35% increase in transparency. SPECS use two Snapdragon processors for computer vision and lens execution, achieving 7ms motion-to-photon latency. They offer up to 4 hours of mixed-use battery life, with an included charging case providing up to 20 total hours. Commerce Kit will power in-lens subscriptions and purchases. SPECS are priced at $2,195 and are available for pre-order at specs.com, expected to ship this fall. Privacy features include clear access requests, an LED light for recording, on-device processing, and user control over data.
