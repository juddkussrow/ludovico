Schiit Vestri Review: I Didn't Believe It Either—Until I Heard It

@octopusinc_ · 2026-07-25 · Hardware

This video reviews the Schiit Vestri, a $99 portable DAC and headphone amplifier, highlighting its proprietary Mesh DAC filter technology. The Vestri bypasses the internal processing of its ES9018 DAC chip, applying Schiit's own filters to achieve superior soundstage, natural tonality, and strong resolution. The review compares the Vestri against several competitors, including the FiiO KA15, FiiO QX13, iFi Hip DAC 3, and xDuoo XA-02, consistently finding the Vestri to offer a more expansive and lifelike sound experience.
