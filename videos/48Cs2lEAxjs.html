SLS 3D printing with TPU offers benefits like shorter iteration cycles, cost-effective customization, and increased geometric flexibility, enabling complex designs like lattices and internal channels that are impossible with traditional tooling. Unlike FDM, which produces anisotropic parts prone to delamination, SLS fuses TPU powder in all directions, resulting in strong, isotropic parts suitable for end-use applications.
