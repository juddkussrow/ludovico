This video, presented by James Buckhouse, a Design Partner at Sequoia Capital and former DreamWorks animator, distills the 3-act story structure into a practical framework. It explains how this structure, encompassing "pain, strain, and gain," is fundamental for creating compelling narratives across various mediums, from film to business pitches. The core idea is that stories are emotional state machines, where characters undergo transformation through choices made in critical moments.
