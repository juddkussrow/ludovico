Can an AI-Optimized Toroidal Propeller Beat a Conventional One?

@octopusinc_ · 2026-09-24 · Hardware

This video explores the potential of AI-optimized toroidal propellers to outperform conventional propellers in efficiency and noise reduction. The creator designs a parametric toroidal propeller using Onshape, optimizes it with a neural network guided by CFD simulations, and then 3D prints and tests the best designs using Formlabs Form 4L. The results indicate that while toroidal propellers offer acoustic benefits, they currently fall short in aerodynamic efficiency compared to conventional designs.
