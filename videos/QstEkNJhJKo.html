The video introduces the "Vault" modular stacking toolbox system, a 3D-printable storage solution. The system is based on the 42x42mm gridfinity unit, with modules available in three widths (S, M, L) and three heights (1, 2, 4 units), and five depth options (3, 4, 5, 6, 7 units), totaling 45 modules. The M module is specifically designed to optimize printing on a Bamboo Lab P1S build plate. The L module is double the size of S, allowing for pairing and assembly. The creators detail a significant design challenge where initial modules bent due to weight distribution. The solution involved moving the load to the sides, sacrificing universal connection for improved stacking stability. They also introduced an XL model, printed at a 45-degree angle to maximize printer output, and later enlarged it to connect with S and M models, also serving as a sustainer. The system includes "sustainers" (drawers) for every size and height, totaling 65 sustainers and 65 drawers (130 units). Strength testing led to reinforced connections with angled new parts and fins to eliminate wiggle. Runners were redesigned to ditch springs for effortless sliding, with side-inserted stoppers and thin panels for variants with one-sided access. The old design of the runners is shown to lack functionality compared to the new design which allows isolation of sections.
