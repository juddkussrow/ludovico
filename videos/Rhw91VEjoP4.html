It can stick to the back of a phone with magnets or a compatible case, demonstrated with a OnePlus 15 and Vivo X300 Pro. The device features a metal frame with rounded corners, a trio of buttons for menus and volume, and dual USB ports (one top, one bottom) for data and pass-through charging. It also includes 3.5mm and 4.4mm audio output ports. The rear has a faux leather texture, and the face has a screen displaying volume, menus, and customizable wallpapers/photos. The MD3 supports UAC 2.0 and UAC 1.0, with a max sampling rate of PCM 32bit/384kHz and DSD256 (Native). It uses ESS Sabre ES9039Q2M DAC chips and ESS ES9603Q x4 amplifier chips. Output power is 180mW @32Ω for 3.5mm and 280mW @32Ω for 4.4mm. Headphone impedance support is 16-300Ω. The device weighs 50g (without cable) and measures 70 x 45 x 12mm. The reviewer notes the convenience of dual USB ports for pass-through charging and flexible cable management, especially when using with a desktop PC to avoid clearance issues with bulky headphone cables. The MD3 provides granular 100-step volume control. When paired with Fosi Audio IM4 earbuds, the volume rarely needed to exceed 30 in high amp output mode. With Meze 105s, 45 was comfortable, and Audeze MM520s needed around 50. Even Beyerdynamic DT 770 Pro 250 ohm headphones were driven to 75, though not optimally. The reviewer's main gripes are the lack of physical music playback controls (play/pause, skip tracks) despite having buttons for photo slideshows, and volume adjustments not turning the screen back on for visual confirmation. Compared to the Shanling UA4 and Onyx Alpha, the MD3 is larger but offers magnets and pass-through charging. The Shanling and Onyx are smaller, lack magnets, offer more customization with EQ presets, and provide more power for thirstier headphones, along with music playback controls. However, the Fosi MD3 has a nicer screen and its lifestyle features make it a better companion for phone users, even if the others are "literally better audiophile solutions." The reviewer also briefly discusses the Fosi Audio IM4 open-ear IEMs, noting their punchy single-driver design with good bass response and included ear tips for tuning. He recommends them for controlled listening environments due to their open-back nature.
