Dip your 3D Prints!

@octopusinc_ · 2022-07-31 · Hardware

This video explores using clear lacquer to achieve a smooth, glossy finish on resin 3D prints, particularly for clear or translucent parts. The creator demonstrates dipping and brushing techniques on various prints, comparing the results to spray paint and highlighting the pros and cons of the lacquer method. The Elegoo Mars 3 3D printer and Elegoo Mercury X Wash & Cure Bundle are used for the printing and post-processing steps.
