The process involves generating multiple views of a subject from a single input video, then converting these views into a 3D Gaussian Splat. The creator explains that 4DAnyone first extracts a 3D skeleton to lock the pose across different angles, and a fine-tuned video model fills in details. The model keeps previously generated angles on hand to ensure consistency and smooth transitions. The 3D Gaussian Splatting technique uses millions of tiny semi-transparent blobs (gaussians) that are adjusted during training to match the original images from various camera angles. The creator highlights two key modifications made to the original pipeline: replacing GVHMR (which uses SMPL-X and has a restrictive research license) with Meta's SAM 3D Body for commercial-friendly skeleton data, and providing an installer for gsplat, which typically requires manual compilation. The ComfyUI workflow is drag-and-drop, requiring users to install two custom nodes and copy two files to set up gsplat. Users input a vertical video (full body or close-up) and download necessary models. Key settings in ComfyUI include camera presets (e.g., "32 views, 2 rings - full body" for highest quality), enabling "Turbo" for faster processing, and activating "low VRAM" for systems with less than 24 GB of VRAM. The video showcases an attempt to recreate the "Matrix bullet time" effect using an iPhone slow-motion video of the creator jumping onto a mattress. While initial results were "not great" due to the challenging nature of the shot (weird poses, loose clothes), a cleanup pass using MiniMax H3 significantly improved the outcome. The creator also discusses integrating the splat sequence into a 3D environment generated using a previous workflow, attempting to use Blender with the 3DGS Render by KIRI Engine add-on, but encountering issues with splat sequence support. Ultimately, a static splat was used in Blender, and Claude was used to merge splats and add effects like bullets. The final shot, after MiniMax H3 cleanup, successfully incorporated a helicopter.
