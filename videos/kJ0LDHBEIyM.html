It contrasts with "vector-only RAG" systems, which are good for fuzzy recall but lack conflict awareness, timelines, and decision history. Semantica offers structured, queryable context, making it suitable for high-stakes domains like finance, healthcare, legal, and government where explainability and auditability are critical.
