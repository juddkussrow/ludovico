Scribe Bets on a New Era of Gene Editing

@octopusinc_ · 2026-07-24 · Bio Gen

Scribe Therapeutics, co-founded by Nobel laureate Jennifer Doudna, is pioneering a new gene-editing technology called "epi editing" that aims to lower LDL cholesterol with a single, non-permanent treatment. This approach is designed to be safer and more broadly applicable than earlier gene-editing methods, making it suitable for common diseases like cardiovascular disease. The company's recent IPO, which raised $128.7 million, will fund the progression of these therapies through clinical trials.
