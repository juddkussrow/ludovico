Introducing umbrelOS 2.0: Your cloud, at home

@octopusinc_ · 2026-09-22 · Hardware

umbrelOS 2.0 is a self-hosted cloud operating system designed to run on various hardware, including mini PCs, NAS devices, Raspberry Pi, or dedicated Umbrel hardware. It offers features like multi-user accounts, AI agent integration, virtual machines, and enhanced storage management with SSD acceleration and FailSafe (RAID). The update also introduces a new Photos app with automatic camera roll backup via an iPhone app and GPU acceleration for AI models and media transcoding.
