The workflow involves `explore` (to think through options), `propose` (to generate the plan as files), and `apply` (to have the AI implement against them). It is tool-agnostic, integrating with over 30 AI assistants (like Claude, Cursor, Copilot) and 25+ developer tools. It supports brownfield projects, cross-repo specs, and an iterative process. The creator emphasizes that OpenSpec provides discipline, not magic; users still need to review AI output and write good specs.
