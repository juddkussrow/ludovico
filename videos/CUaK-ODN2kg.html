How To Make Your Own Foil/Holographic Trading Cards At Home!

@octopusinc_ · 2021-01-29 · Hardware

This video demonstrates a DIY method for creating holographic trading cards using a standard inkjet printer, acetate sheets, and holographic cardstock. The process involves designing card artwork in software like Photoshop or Pixlr, printing the design onto transparent acetate, and then adhering the acetate to holographic paper before cutting out the cards. The tutorial emphasizes leaving white areas in the design for the holographic effect to show through.
