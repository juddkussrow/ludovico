The World’s Largest Air Battery Just Switched On

@octopusinc_ · 2026-09-15 · Hardware

This video analyzes the efficacy of China's newly built compressed air battery, the world's largest, located in a salt cavern in Huai'an. It compares this technology to lithium-ion batteries and other long-duration storage solutions like Energy Dome's CO2 battery and Hydrostor's Willow Rock project. The analysis reveals that while compressed air offers a structural advantage for long-duration storage due to its separate pump and tank costs, China's current implementation only provides 4 hours of storage, similar to lithium, making it more of a peaker plant replacement than a true long-duration solution.
