The "Masking" section covers the "circle mask" for hiding soft fisheye rims, with presets for rigs like DJI Osmo 360, Insta360 X5, and Antigravity A1. "Keyword masking" uses SAM 3 with text prompts (e.g., "person, phone") and a detection threshold to mask elements, allowing users to adjust confidence levels. A "paintbrush" tool enables manual masking on single or all frames, and on both lenses simultaneously. The "Alignment" stage involves two steps: Stage one uses COLMAP Structure from Motion on raw fisheye frames to recover camera poses, and Stage two renders pinhole tiles and re-triangulates a denser cloud. "Reuse fisheye features" speeds up densification but can soften details. Matcher options include "Sequential" (default for video), "Exhaustive" (most thorough, slow), and "Spatial" (uses GPS neighbors). Overlap settings determine how many neighboring frames are compared (17 for raw fisheye, 10 for stitched 360). "Loop detection" corrects drift by matching frames far apart in time. "Lens priors" provide COLMAP with known focal length and distortion, with an auto option for self-calibration. "GLOMAP global mapper" solves all camera poses at once for faster processing, but an incremental mapper is available if the model is misaligned. "Geometry Timeline Check" is an experimental feature for dual fisheye to prevent physical inconsistencies in walkthroughs. "Preset quality" adjusts quality vs. speed vs. VRAM, with options for "Low" (4GB cards, quick previews), "Default" (COLMAP defaults), and "High" (denser cloud, slower). "Add detail photos" allows integrating external photos (DSLR, drone, phone) for sharpening. Advanced settings open a CLI drawer for fine-tuning.
