How to Generate & Rig 3D Models: ComfyUI (Free) vs. Atlas AI (Pro)

@octopusinc_ · 2026-07-30 · Favorites, Story, Tutorial, Up Next, Video Gen

This video compares two methods for generating and rigging 3D models from character sheets: a free, local setup using ComfyUI, Blender, and AccuRig, and a paid, cloud-based solution called Atlas Studio. The creator demonstrates the complex, multi-step process with ComfyUI and its Trellis 2 extension, highlighting challenges with texturing and the need for external tools like Blender for format conversion and AccuRig for rigging. In contrast, Atlas Studio offers a more streamlined, AI-assisted workflow that generates and rigs models with fewer steps and higher quality.
