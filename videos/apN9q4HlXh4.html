Aurral: 1.1M Pulls for Self-Hosted Music Discovery [Free]

@octopusinc_ · 2026-09-01 · Hardware, Music Gen

Aurral is an open-source, self-hosted music discovery companion for Lidarr that provides personalized recommendations, rotating playlists, and release tracking. It integrates with existing self-hosted music stacks like Lidarr, Navidrome, and Plex, allowing users to regain discovery features similar to commercial streaming services while maintaining ownership of their music library. The tool is deployed as a single Docker service and supports multi-user environments with various import and download options.
