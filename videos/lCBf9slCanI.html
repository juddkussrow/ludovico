Taste Labs, founded by Thais Castello Branco, aims to end "AI slop" by improving AI's ability to handle subjective domains like design and creative writing. The core approach involves decomposing subjective problems into verifiable elements and building high-quality, structured preference data through expert human QA. This method helps train models to move beyond average outputs and reward creativity, addressing the "collapse to the mean" problem in AI-generated content.
