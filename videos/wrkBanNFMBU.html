The series includes 11 distinct CPU flavors across 18 boards, categorized into four main branches: Espressif (connected generalists), Nordic Semiconductor (wireless specialists), Raspberry Pi (software first), and "Specialists" (Renesas and Silicon Labs). The XIAO SAMD21, the original board, uses an ARM Cortex M0+ and has no wireless. The ESP32 family includes the ESP32C3 (cheapest wireless, RISC-V, 160MHz, Wi-Fi 4, Bluetooth 5.0), ESP32C6 (RISC-V, Wi-Fi 6, Bluetooth LE, Zigbee, Thread, Matter support), ESP32S3 (Xtensa LX7 dual-core, 240MHz, 8MB PSRAM, 8MB Flash, Wi-Fi 4, Bluetooth 5, good for ML), ESP32S3 Sense (adds OV3660 camera, PDM mic, microSD), ESP32S3 Plus (double flash, 9 additional GPIOs), and ESP32C5 (RISC-V, 8MB PSRAM/Flash, dual-band Wi-Fi 2.4/5GHz, Matter, Zigbee, Thread). Nordic boards include nRF52840 (Cortex-M4F, 64MHz, BLE, NFC, low power, Sense/Plus variants available) and nRF54L15 (Cortex-M33, 128MHz, RISC-V security coprocessor, BLE 6.0, Thread, Zigbee, Amazon Sidewalk, Sense variant available). The nRF52840 LM 28 adds Apple Find My and Google Find My Device. Nordic boards use nRF Connect SDK and Zephyr, lacking Arduino IDE support. Raspberry Pi boards are RP2040 (dual-core ARM Cortex-M0+, 133MHz, 264KB SRAM, 2MB Flash, no wireless, good for Python) and RP2350 (dual Cortex-M33 or two ESP32 RISC-V cores, 150MHz, 520KB SRAM, 2MB Flash, 19 GPIOs, secure boot). Specialist boards include RA4M1 (ARM Cortex M0+, 133MHz, 5V tolerant pins, 14-bit ADC, 12-bit DAC, CAN bus) and MG24 (ARM Cortex-M33, 78MHz, 256KB RAM, 1.5MB NVM Flash, Matter native, deep sleep under 2uA, Sense variant available). The XIAO ecosystem also features an Expansion Base with OLED, RTC, microSD, buzzer, Grove connectors, and battery management, compatible with the Grove ecosystem of plug-and-play modules.
