This video showcases the deployment of Anduril's Sentry Towers by U.S. Customs and Border Protection along the Southwest border as part of the Autonomous Surveillance Towers (AST) program. The Sentry Towers are autonomous, solar-powered surveillance systems designed for rapid deployment in remote areas without requiring extensive infrastructure. They integrate with Anduril's Lattice software platform to provide real-time situational awareness to border patrol agents.
