ALOGIC Aspekt Ultrawide Touch 34 Review

@octopusinc_ · 2026-08-18 · Hardware

This video reviews the ALOGIC Aspekt Ultrawide Touch 34 monitor, highlighting its unique adjustable stand that allows it to be pulled down into a drawing position, similar to the Microsoft Surface Studio. The reviewer praises the stand's functionality and the monitor's ultrawide aspect ratio for general use, but criticizes the display quality, resolution, and pen performance for professional illustration, especially given its high price point. The monitor is presented as a good option for note-taking or photo editing, but not for professional artists who rely on precise line work.
