HeyGears Reflex 2 Pro: From Prototype to Production

@octopusinc_ · 2025-10-23 · Hardware

The HeyGears Reflex 2 Pro is a resin 3D printer designed for production-level manufacturing, moving beyond prototyping. It features an OptiZone light engine with over 1,000 individually controlled mini LEDs for precise curing, an Amber Screen Pro with enhanced UV resistance and double the light transmission of conventional LCD screens, and an industrial Z-axis with FOC motor for micron-level accuracy and high-speed batch production. The printer also includes intelligent features like auto-resin refilling, residue detection, and auto-leveling for reliable, continuous operation.
